About Wildflower Canines

Consider how the wildflowers grow.

Wildflowers aren't rushed or forced into bloom. They grow when the light, the soil, and the season are right. I believe dogs are the same, and that belief shapes everything I do.

Bri & Lemon
My story

It started with Lemon.

The dog at the center of our logo is Lemon, and Lemon is the one who taught me the most. Not about commands, but about patience, listening, and what trust really looks like between a dog and a person.

That lesson became Wildflower Canines: a Las Vegas training and boarding company built on the idea that every dog grows in its own time and its own way.

Today I work with dogs of every size, age, and energy level, from wiggly puppies to strong-willed working breeds. The goal is always the same: a calmer, more confident dog and an owner who feels capable at the other end of the leash.

Our name
“Consider how the wild flowers grow.”
Luke 12:27

That verse is where the name comes from. Rather than forcing obedience, we focus on patient, individualized care: building confidence step by step, session by session. Some dogs need more structure. Others just need someone to finally listen. Either way, we give each one the conditions to grow.

What guides us

A shared language, rooted in respect.

Our mission

We recognize the uniqueness of every family and their companion, and tailor training to your specific needs. By working together, we make the experience enriching for both you and your dog, building a lasting, joyful relationship.

Our vision

Training that puts empathy ahead of obedience. We work to understand your dog's challenges while honoring their unique spirit, so together we can grow a shared language rooted in grace and mutual respect.

What you can expect
