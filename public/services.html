Private Lessons

One-on-one sessions tailored to your dog's specific needs and goals, whether you're working through a single challenge or building a full foundation together.

  • You want to learn alongside your dog, hands on
  • There's a specific behavior you'd like help with
  • You're starting a puppy off on the right paw

Training · 02

Day Training

Your dog comes to us for focused training sessions during the day and goes home to you every night, so the bond stays at home while the skills get built alongside us.

  • Your schedule is full, but you want real progress
  • You'd like your dog home with you each evening
  • You want skills built by us, then handed off to you

Training · 03

Board and Train

An immersive training experience where your dog stays with us to build lasting habits and confidence in a structured environment, with follow-up lessons included to carry it all home.

  • Your dog would benefit from daily structure and consistency
  • You want a strong foundation in a shorter window
  • Follow-up lessons help you keep the progress going

Boarding For existing training clients

A familiar place to stay.

Boarding is offered only to existing training clients, on a case-by-case basis. Your dog stays somewhere they already know, with someone who already knows them, and their training stays on track while you're away.
